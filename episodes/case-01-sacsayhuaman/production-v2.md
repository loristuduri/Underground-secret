# Production v2 — Chinkanas

A 40-second vertical edit was rendered locally as `UNDERGROUND_SECRET_CASE01_CHINKANAS_ARCHIVES_V2.mp4`.

## Edit contents
- Licensed moving archive footage of the surface site (Wikimedia Commons, 2011, Pierre André Leclercq, CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Two 2026 Wikimedia Commons photographs of Chincana Chica: Rutihsoc (CC BY 4.0) and Ffranco97 (CC BY-SA 4.0), animated with camera push-ins.
- Original schematic route graphic and georadar-style explanatory illustration; both labelled non-scale / illustrative, not field measurements.
- Voice reused from 00:53–01:30 of the existing 90-second project video.

## Key framing
Chincana Chica is shown as a real site feature. A continuous route to Qoricancha is presented as a research claim under investigation, not a proven fact. The 2011 moving footage shows the surface site and is not drone footage.
