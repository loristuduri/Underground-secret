## Editorial guardrails
- The Chincana Chica photographs depict real site features. They are not evidence by themselves of a continuous tunnel to Qoricancha.
- The route graphic is expressly schematic and not to scale; its question mark marks an investigatory claim.
- The georadar-style panel is an original explanatory illustration, not a radar result or measured site data.
- The 2011 moving archive footage shows the surface site, not a drone flight and not a subsurface excavation.
- No paid drone footage has been licensed or included. Drone clips found on stock platforms require a separate purchase; openly shared aerial survey images from the University of Arkansas collection carry a noncommercial restriction and were excluded.
